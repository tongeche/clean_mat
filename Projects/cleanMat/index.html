Frequently asked questions

What services does CleanMat offer?

CleanMat connects you with various laundry service providers offering wash & fold, dry cleaning, ironing, and specialized care for delicate items.

How do I schedule a pickup?

Simply click the "Schedule a Pickup" button, enter your details, choose your preferred time, and have your laundry ready for the driver.

Are there any hidden fees?

No, CleanMat is transparent about pricing. All costs are clearly displayed before you confirm your service.

Can I specify my laundry preferences?

Yes, when placing your order, you can add notes for specific preferences, such as detergent type, folding style, or any special instructions for delicate items.

How long does the service take?

Most standard wash & fold services are completed within 24-48 hours. Dry cleaning and specialized services may take longer, as specified by the provider.

Is my laundry insured?

Reputable laundry providers on CleanMat typically offer insurance for your garments. Please check the specific provider's terms for details.
